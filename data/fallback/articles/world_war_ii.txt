World War II or the Second World War (1939–1945) was a global conflict involving most of the world's nations. It was the deadliest conflict in human history. The war began on 1 September 1939 when Germany invaded Poland. The two main opposing military alliances were the Allies and the Axis powers. Key Allied leaders included Franklin D. Roosevelt, Winston Churchill, and Joseph Stalin. Adolf Hitler led Nazi Germany, the primary Axis power in Europe. The war in Europe ended with the German surrender on 8 May 1945 (VE Day). The war in the Pacific ended after atomic bombs were dropped on Hiroshima and Nagasaki in August 1945, leading to Japan's surrender on 2 September 1945 (VJ Day). An estimated 70 to 85 million people died in the conflict.
